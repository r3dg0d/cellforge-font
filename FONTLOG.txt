FONTLOG for Cellforge
================
An original pixel-grid type family: 9 weights × upright and pixel-step italic, built procedurally from hand-drawn pixel maps.

Basic Font Information
----------------------
Generated procedurally by tools/engine.py from sources/pixelmaps.txt.
Engine: pixel map → weighted pixel rectangles + bridges + stepped diagonals → pathops union.
